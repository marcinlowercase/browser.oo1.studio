Account & Data Deletion

Instructions for permanently deleting your cloud sync data and revoking access for the browser of oo1 studio by oo1 studio.

the browser of oo1 studio operates on a serverless, local-first Bring-Your-Own-Storage (BYOS) architecture. oo1 studio does not host or operate centralized database servers; all cloud backups reside exclusively inside your personal Google Drive account. You have full self-serve control to permanently purge your data at any time.

1. In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have the app installed, you can permanently erase your entire cloud backup directly through the browser:

  • Open the browser of oo1 studio on your device.
  • Open the browser settings. If not already signed in, tap "Sign in with Google" to authenticate your session.
  • Once authenticated, the button displays as the "Sync" button. Tap it to open the Sync Panel.
  • Tap the "Delete Account" button and confirm the dialog.

Upon confirmation, the app immediately communicates with the Google Drive API to permanently delete all synced backup files and folders from your Google Drive, and then automatically signs you out.

2. Deletion Without the App (If Uninstalled)

If you have already uninstalled the application and wish to permanently revoke access and delete your stored data without reinstalling, follow these self-guided steps:

  • Revoke Access: Open your Google Account third-party connections at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Locate and select "the browser of oo1 studio" from your list of connected apps.
  • Click "Delete all connections" (or "Revoke Access") to instantly sever the browser's access to your Google account.
  • Remove Stored Files: Open Google Drive, locate the browser application folder (or search for backup payloads), and delete it. Then empty your Google Drive Trash.

3. What Data Gets Deleted?

Completing either deletion process permanently erases the following data:

  • All synchronized browser profiles and profile names.
  • All synced pinned web applications, local app HTML code, and custom icons.
  • All synced browsing history and visited URL records.
  • All synced browser preferences (such as corner radius, padding, and search engine settings).

Data Retention: Deletion is immediate and permanent. Because oo1 studio does not operate central servers, no copies or residual backups are retained anywhere on our systems.

4. Removing Local Device Data

Cloud deletion does not affect data stored locally in your device's internal application storage. To erase all local cookies, cache, tabs, and on-device history:

  • Use the built-in "Clear Site Data" or "Close All Tabs" options inside the browser settings, or
  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → the browser of oo1 studio → Storage → "Clear Data", or
  • Uninstall the application directly from your Android device.
